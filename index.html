Security & Privacy

Privacy Architecture

Zero tracking. Zero telemetry. 100% local execution.

1. Zero Data Collection: We do not track, collect, or transmit your URLs, search terms, IP address, or browsing history.

2. Local Storage Only: All configuration settings (such as clock format and theme preferences) are stored strictly within Chrome's local storage on your machine.

3. No External Analytics: The codebase contains no third-party tracking scripts, telemetry pixels, or marketing beacons.
