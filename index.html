Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you. Period.

1. Zero Data Collection: Premium Wallpaper Tab does not collect, track, transmit, or monetize your search queries, URLs, IP address, or browser history.

2. Local Storage Only: All preferences — such as your favorite wallpapers, clock format (12h/24h), and brightness dimming — are stored exclusively inside your browser's local storage.

3. No External Analytics: We do not inject tracking cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party marketing tags.

4. Image CDN: Wallpaper imagery is requested directly from Unsplash's public high-availability CDN with standard HTTPS encryption.
